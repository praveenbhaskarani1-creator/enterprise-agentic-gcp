# Returns and Refunds Policy

A customer may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery.

Unopened standard products are eligible for review. Defective products must be
escalated to a human support specialist even when the package has been opened.

After a human approves a refund, the original payment method normally receives
the credit within 5 to 10 business days.

The Customer Operations Agent may explain this policy and retrieve relevant
customer information. It must not approve refunds, change payment records, or
promise that a refund will be issued.
