# Support Escalation Runbook

Priority 1 includes a production outage, suspected security incident, or
widespread inability to access the service. Human acknowledgment is required
within 15 minutes.

Priority 2 includes a blocked business workflow or an issue affecting multiple
customers without a complete outage. Human acknowledgment is required within
one business hour.

Priority 3 includes an isolated, non-blocking issue. Human acknowledgment is
required within one business day.

Refund approval, payment modification, account closure, and privileged data
export always require an authorized human decision. The agent may collect
context and recommend escalation but must not execute these actions.
